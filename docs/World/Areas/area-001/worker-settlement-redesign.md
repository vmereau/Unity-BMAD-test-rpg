# Worker settlement redesign — proposal

Status: proposed, 2026-11-11. The current area document and scene describe the existing blockout; this proposal becomes authoritative when approved and implemented.

## Construction and social hierarchy

The camp has lasted long enough for workers to build with timber offcuts. Solid shelter is common, but dry floors, privacy, sound roofs and space are privileges. Rough split-plank huts and half-walled frames distinguish workers from the Company's maintained buildings. Canvas fills the spaces construction has not reached.

Keep the current zones. Arrange housing in small groups around shared outdoor rooms, not uniform rows. Two or three huts anchor each group; one intervening tarp bay holds overflow sleepers and belongings. Keep fires in uncovered yards. Rope and tarp anchors should attach to posts or hut frames, with drainage directed away from entrances.

## Walkways and important views

- Preserve the 5 m Company Road, the punishment square and all three 6 m sight lanes. No tarp bridges across these corridors.
- Target at least 2.5 m clear for principal pedestrian routes and 1.5 m for short secondary passages, measured between props and posts. Widen where the third-person camera needs it.
- Use short boardwalks at entrances, muddy crossings and the canteen. Leave visible dirt between them rather than decking the whole camp.
- Make each yard visible from its approach through a gap between huts. Shared fires remain under open sky.
- Keep the player's rear view through BrokenStretch, the warehouse ramp approach, both ditch crossings and the night route to the gang house open.
- Do not fill the lakeside or expand the housing into the pit, Company compound or work areas.
- Retain functional beds, routine spots and interactables as hand-owned objects; housing must accommodate their access.

## Artwork briefs

**Overhead study:** Environment layout study for a dark medieval fantasy RPG in the style of Gothic 1 and 2. Preserve the reference's zone boundaries, roads, gates, punishment square, ditch and shoreline. Replace most worker tents with clusters of small split-plank huts, pitch-stained timber frames and patched undyed linen tarps spanning selected gaps. Visible bedrolls beneath side shelters. Poor partial walls north-west, shared worker courtyards in the middle, better enclosed huts east of the ditch. Timber cookhouse with an open eating canopy west of the square. A few full tents remain. Clear open routes between clusters, uncovered fire yards, short uneven plank walkways. Muted earthy palette, overcast daylight, realistic construction and readable roof footprints.

**Ground-level study:** Environment concept for a dark medieval fantasy RPG in the style of Gothic 1 and 2. Human eye level from a worker's small shared courtyard toward a timber canteen. Rough split-plank huts close together, one patched linen tarp between side walls sheltering bedrolls and sacks, an uncovered fire yard and a repair chest. Uneven boards lead onto a clear dirt walking route. Canteen serving hatch and open-sided eating canopy in the middle distance. Weathered wood, stained cloth, packed mud, muted earthy palette and overcast daylight. Buildings feel incrementally assembled and cramped; entrances and the walking route remain readable.
