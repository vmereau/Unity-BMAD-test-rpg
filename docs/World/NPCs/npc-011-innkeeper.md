## Identity

Aldric Sorn ran a modest inn in the capital before a failed deal left him owing debts to people who do not forgive. He relocated to the outskirts, opened the Creaking Flagon, and quietly rebuilt — this time trading in information and discreet services rather than just ale. Friendly by habit and dangerous by necessity, he has survived long enough to know that patience and a warm smile outlast most weapons.

---

## Role in Area

Runs the Creaking Flagon, the player's base for rest and resupply. Offers services (rest, trade) on the surface; can teach niche skills and provide rumours to players who earn his trust. Functions as an early ambient hint that not everyone in the starting area is what they appear.

---

## Personality

**Tone:** Warm and welcoming on the surface; careful and measured underneath
**Vocabulary:** Common folk speech, occasionally too precise for a simple innkeeper
**Quirks:** Wipes the bar even when clean; answers questions with questions; never volunteers information
**Taboo topics:** His life before the Flagon; what happens in the cellar; who he reports to
**Sample line:** "You look like you've had a long road. Sit, drink — and tell me what's on your mind."
