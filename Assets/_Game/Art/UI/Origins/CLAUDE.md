## Prompt template

Generated by the user (text-to-image or image-to-image), never by Claude. Keep every line **verbatim**: only
`{SCENE}` changes, which is what makes the set read as one. `{SCENE}` = when (before the war / during it), the
setting, his clothing (material, fit, one wear cue), what his hands do, the belongings in view, then the light.
The belongings must match the origin's `promisedItems`: the picture sells what the Company will take.

```
Painted illustration for a dark medieval fantasy RPG in the style of Gothic 1 and 2, vertical 3:4 portrait format.
{REFERENCE}
A realistic adult man about 30 years old, lean and wiry with realistic proportions, a weathered commoner, not a hero and not a bodybuilder. Short cropped dark brown hair close to the scalp, short stubble beard, a plain tired face.
{SCENE}
He is shown at medium-full length, from the knees up, slightly off-center, absorbed in his work, not looking at the viewer, not posing.
Realistic painterly rendering with visible brushwork, grounded and lived-in, every object made, used and repaired.
Muted earthy palette of browns, stone grays, mossy greens and cold blue shadows; warm firelight is the only strong accent. No saturated colors, no glow effects, no pure black, no pure white.
Soft atmospheric depth, natural light only, no rim light, no lens flare, no bloom.
No text, no title, no frame, no border, no watermark.
```

- `{REFERENCE}`: empty for text-to-image. With `Art/Characters/Humanoids/models/test hero front view.png` attached:
  `Same man as the reference image: same face, hair, beard, skin tone and body proportions.`
- Wretch: `absorbed in his work` → `absorbed in the moment`.
- Negative field (if the tool has one): `glowing, magical, epic, heroic, ornate, shiny, vibrant, saturated colors,
  armor, weapons, cape, text, watermark, frame, cartoon, anime, 3D render`.

## Import and wiring

1. Save as `Origin_<Id>.png`, 3:4, ≥ 896×1200.
2. Importer: Sprite (2D and UI), Single, no mipmaps, Max Size 2048, Compressed HQ, Clamp, Bilinear.
3. Assign the sprite to `OriginSO._artwork` (`Data/Origins/Origin_<Id>.asset`). A null artwork hides the image.
4. Layout: `MainMenu.unity` → `Window/OriginPick/Artwork` (left of `Details`, `preserveAspect`, saved inactive:
   an Image with no sprite draws a white box). Sizes at the current 1000×920 Window: Artwork 324×432 (stretches
   with the panel height), `Details` 512 wide, font 26 with auto-size down to 18. The Blacksmith text uses 388 of
   432 px at 26.
