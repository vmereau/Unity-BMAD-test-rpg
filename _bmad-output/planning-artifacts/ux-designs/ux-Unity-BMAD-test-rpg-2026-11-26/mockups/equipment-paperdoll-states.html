Equipment: paper doll, the chosen direction in three states

The 1920×1080 Inventory screen at 50%. The doll panel is 496×800 on the left. A plain silhouette stands in the middle, with seven 88×88 slots each joined by a dashed ink line to the body part it covers. Left side, top to bottom: Helmet, Armor, Weapon, Ring 1. Right side: Necklace, Offhand, Ring 2. The rings sit by the hands. There is no stat summary: stats stay on the detail panel. Slot states: brass = selected, warm = hovered, moss = valid place for the item you hold or have selected (that colour is my proposal, change it if you like), and faded icon = offhand taken by a two-handed weapon.

1Default: an equipped item selected

Clicking the Armor slot selects it with the same brass frame as a grid slot, and the shared detail panel shows the Leather Armor with Unequip and Drop. The Necklace and Ring 2 slots are empty: a dark well labelled with the slot name. The line from the selected slot is drawn in the same brass.

2A ring selected in the grid: both ring slots light up

Selecting (or starting to drag) an equippable item in the grid lights up every slot it can go into in moss green. Both ring slots light up here, and Equip would put the ring in Ring 2 because it is the first empty one. Hovering a slot gives it the warm edge. Lines to slots the item can't go into stay plain ink.

3Two-handed weapon equipped, a shield dragged over the Offhand slot

The Flamberge takes both hands, so the Offhand slot shows its icon faded and both hand lines point to it. While the kite shield is dragged, the Offhand slot lights up as a valid target. Dropping it there swaps: the shield is equipped and the two-handed weapon goes back to the grid, by the rules the equipment system already applies. Ring 2 is empty, so this frame has no second ring.
