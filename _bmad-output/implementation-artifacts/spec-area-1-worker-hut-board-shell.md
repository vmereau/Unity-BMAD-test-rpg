## Intent

**Problem:** The worker hut shells are assembled from kit pieces of three packs. Pass 2 matched their tone, but each piece keeps its own texture, grain, board width and texel density, so the roof, walls and the custom roof poles still don't read as one building.

**Approach:** Replace the kit shell with a hut modelled in Blender board by board (a script, so both sizes come from it), on one shared tileable wood material reused from the kit's textures. The hut builder keeps the shell contract (footprint, openings, colliders, restricted area) and swaps the kit pieces for the new model. Targets: `H1 home yard day`, `H7 hut roof poles`.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** Follow `ART_DIRECTION.md` and the Camp custom-piece rules (real size, 1 m per tile box UVs, −Z forward / Y up / Apply Transform, `Source~/`). Keep the shell contract of `Huts/CLAUDE.md` unchanged: +Z doorway at local x 0 to 1.05, 2.14 m high; rear opening x −0.45 to 0.4, 1.05 to 1.95 m up; eave 2.5 m; wall centre lines 3 × 3 m (Home) and 3 × 4 m (Long); room 2.56 m between inner faces; no floor; `Colliders`, `Roof` (Not Walkable) and `RestrictedArea` keep their names and paths. Both prefabs stay generated by `Tools/World/Build Worker Hut Kit Prefabs`. Every wood surface of the shell, and the weight poles, use materials that share one texture set. Rebake the NavMesh after the rebuild.

**Ask First:** No kit wood texture tiles cleanly enough, so a new texture would be needed. Any change to the contract's dimensions or to a scene position. Any Meshy or image generation.

**Never:** Meshy / `generate_image` / OpenRouter in this spec; editing kit materials or kit prefabs; other huts, zones or the graybox camp; terrain; changing the dressing's content (only what must follow the new shell).

## Spec Change Log

- 2026-11-25, implementation (no loopback; **needs the user's sign-off**, see the summary): the frozen **Always** asks for "1 m per tile box UVs". The kit texture chosen under the user's "reuse" answer, `T_ModularWood` (the side walls' own), holds ten planks per tile with a dark seam between them, so a box projection draws seams across every board. Each board is instead mapped onto one plank, grain along the board, at 2 m per tile (one plank = 0.2 m, no board wider). One grain scale everywhere, no new texture, so **Ask First** was not triggered. Also changed while implementing: the wall colliders keep their names and paths but are 0.2 m thick, from 1.35 m (the old inner face) to 1.55 m, instead of 0.3 m to 1.65 m, because the new wall ends at 1.455 m; the pole lashings moved from over the old gable boards to the barge boards at the roof's ends; the builder takes the model apart into the prefab's own `Walls` and `Roof/RoofBoards` (no nested model prefab). KEEP: one plank per board, boards inside and posts outside, the builder's name and menu.
- 2026-11-25, review (blind hunter, edge-case hunter, acceptance auditor; patches only, no loopback). Fixed: the wall colliders were centred 5 cm outside the wall line instead of inside (1.45 to 1.65 m: the player could sink 17 cm into the boards) — sign corrected, rebuilt, rebaked; the two gable boards at the ridge came out up to 0.4 m wide (two plank seams along one board) — the gable now uses the same splitter as the walls; a plank was 0.18 m while boards went up to 0.22 m — tile set to 2 m and every board capped at 0.2 m (largest V span measured after the fix: one plank); gable boards cut to the roof line kept 1 m of grain whatever their height — UVs are now taken after the cut; the roof board loop could leave up to 5 cm bare at the front gable with other seeds — the whole length is split, the last board runs to the barge board; the lower purlin ran inside the side walls' top boards — moved up the slope; the tie beams' ends lay in the top plates' outer faces — they now stop at the corner posts; side boards could leave a 3 mm slit in the inside corners — their inner face stays at or inside the end boards' ends; door posts leaned up to 12 mm into the doorway — plumb; the material check accepted any `M_Camp_*` material — it now wants the two shell materials; degenerate wall bands and a clashing `Walls` / `Roof` object name in the Blender session are refused; dressing: crate table 5 cm in from the boards, door flap against the lintel, lean-to eave ropes tied round the roof edge instead of through a board; docs: collider numbers, run instructions, plank width, the root and `Prefabs` index lines that still said "kit pieces". Logged, not changed: the `.blend` holds the built objects and materials only (the script is the source); `_BaseColor` values above 1 must be set by script or in the `.mat` (documented). Rejected: none of the reported findings.

## Design Notes

Boards are separate boxes, each mapped onto one plank of the texture (see the change log) with a random shift along the grain, so no two neighbours show the same knots and nothing needs a unique texture. The silhouette does the work the kit textures could not: uneven roof board ends, a sagging ridge log, posts proud of the wall boards.

The builder keeps its name and menu path (other docs and the dresser point at them) although the shell is no longer made of kit pieces.
