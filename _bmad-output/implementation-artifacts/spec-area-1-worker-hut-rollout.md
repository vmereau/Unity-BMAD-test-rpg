## Intent

**Problem:** Only three huts of the camp are the approved board-built shell (`WorkerHut_Home`); the other 36 of `PlayersZone` and the 8 of `MafiaZone` are graybox boxes. Three graybox tarps remain on the east side of the home yard (`worker-tarp-north`, `(4)`, `(5)`), and the lean-to is still tied to a graybox wall.

**Approach:** Export seed variants of the shell (other roof gaps and board ends, one with a mirrored doorway, a tight-eave roof for packed rows) and swap every 3 × 3 m graybox hut of the two zones in place with a rerunnable tool. Then the home yard dresser replaces the three tarps with pass-2 cloth: a tarp strung between the eaves of `worker-hut` and `worker-hut-06`, and one merged shelter for `(4)` + `(5)` tied to huts 05 / 06 and the warehouse ramp.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** Shell contract of `Huts/CLAUDE.md` (sizes, openings, colliders, `Roof` Not Walkable, `RestrictedArea` name and path; a mirrored doorway mirrors x only). Every prefab stays generated by `Tools/World/Build Worker Hut Kit Prefabs` from `build_worker_hut.py`; one wood (`M_Camp_HutShell*`, `M_Camp_PoleWood`). A swap keeps the hut's name, parent, sibling order, x, z and yaw, and seats it on the ground. The variant is chosen from the hut's name, so a rerun gives the same camp. A tight roof is never larger than the graybox roof it replaces. `WorkerHut_Long` only where the extra metre is free of colliders, lanes and a 0.6 m walking gap. Tarps and ropes come from `CampClothMesh`, inside `HomeYardDressing`; beds are sleepable `Bed_FloorPallet`; any walk-under edge is at least 2.1 m high; nothing solid within 0.7 m of a `HutLanes` segment or in front of a doorway. Rebake the NavMesh.

**Ask First:** Moving or turning any hut; terrain edits; a hut that cannot be swapped by the rules below and is not simply left graybox; Meshy or image generation; anything in `Core.unity`.

**Never:** `WretchesCorner` hovels, `Canteen`, `MafiaQuarters` and the boss buildings, the other graybox tarps (`worker-tarp-south`, `north (2)`, `north (6)`, mafia, wretches); hut interiors; NPCs, routines, owners; `generate_image` / OpenRouter; editing kit materials.

## Spec Change Log

- 2026-11-26, implementation (no loopback; **the first two points need the user's sign-off**, see the summary).
  (1) Acceptance criterion 5 ("no roof board of one hut passes through another hut's wall") is **not met**: the camp's plots stand 2.9–3.8 m apart, so even the tight roof (no larger than the graybox one) crosses 33 neighbouring shells, about half of them into the neighbour's wall line, as the graybox roofs did. Closing them means moving huts, which is **Ask First**; nothing was moved. (2) The frozen **Always** says "the variant is chosen from the hut's name": the name's hash sets the order the allowed shells are tried in, and among them the one the fewest neighbours within 4.5 m already show is taken. Deterministic for a given camp, but a single hut redone later can get another shell than a full run would give it. This is what makes "no more than two neighbouring huts in a row" hold (measured: no two swapped huts within 4.5 m share a shell), though it is likely, not enforced. Also changed while implementing: every plot is crowded, so all 40 swaps are tight shells and two more tight seeds were added (`WorkerHut_Tight_C`, `_Tight_D`: eight prefabs, not six); `Home_B`, `Home_C` and `Long` exist and are placed nowhere; three Mafia huts (`worker-hut-28*`) are narrower boxes (walls on ±1.29 m, not the 3 × 3 m of the Code Map) and stay graybox with `worker-hut-02 (9)` (0.34 m drop); the "one merged shelter" is two sheets meeting at hut-06's corner (porch + ramp bay), with no pole; `WorkerHutRolloutRules` is public (the test assembly has no access to internals). KEEP: swap in place with one undo step, tight eaves, eave ties instead of poles, the rules as pure code with tests.
- 2026-11-26, review (blind hunter, edge-case hunter, acceptance auditor; patches only, no loopback). Fixed: the swap deleted the market hut's `TradingCounter` (a non-graybox child of `worker-hut-30`) — restored on the ground inside the shell, and the tool now moves any such child into the shell and lists it (`KEPT`), and refuses a hut with a component on its root; the dresser switched the three graybox tarps off even when their replacement was skipped — each goes off only once its tarp is built; the ramp bay could leave two ropes without a tarp — both ramp points are found first; a tarp could be tied to an eave that does not face its neighbour (gable side) or to the ramp's far side — `EaveFacing`, the ramp's nearest point, and height / distance guards in `RampEdge`; the lean-to accepted a scaled or inactive hut-02 and could collapse its outer edge — guarded, falls back to the old line; the drop was measured over the whole footprint instead of under the walls; a doorway facing a bank counted as free (the terrain is no collider hit) — ground sampled outside it; characters, loose items and `HomeYardDressing` counted as permanent blockers; the three replaced tarps were ignored by bare name in any zone; the swap shared its undo group with earlier edits and left it open on an exception; `ROOFS CROSS` skipped pairs with a graybox hut (now 33 shell pairs + 21 with a graybox hut); the rollout now refuses to run when a rules name is missing from `Variants`; tests: the hash order is asserted against `Hash % 3` for six names (the old test passed without the rotation); docs and comments: lean-to behaviour, hut-07, why the five lone huts are tight, lane distances 4 cm tighter. Logged, not changed: the eave ropes are straight segments from under the boards to just above them (same as the approved lean-to); the lane chain of the long-hut test stops at `HutLane_*_6` (no long hut is placed); the builder keeps its per-build statics (reset at the top of every `Build`). Rejected: new `.cs.meta` files without a `MonoImporter` block (every script meta of this project is the two-line form Unity 6 writes), a null `Prop` (missing pieces return a placeholder), roof-reach numbers (the code's 1.70 is the conservative bound of the measured 1.69).

## Design Notes

The shell's roof is 19 cm wider per side than the graybox one, and most plots already touched: the tight variant trims the overhang to the graybox size (about 0.12 m), so the rollout never makes a row worse than it is today. Mafia huts lose their graybox plank floor (the shell has none); a richer Mafia variant is deferred.
