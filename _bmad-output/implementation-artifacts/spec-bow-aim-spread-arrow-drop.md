## Intent

**Problem:** A nocked bow shot is always pixel-perfect, whatever the aim time. Arrows also fly in a dead-straight line forever.

**Approach:** While nocked, an accuracy cone shrinks from `aimSpreadMaxAngle` (5°) to 0° over `aimSpreadShrinkDuration` (1.5 s). Moving widens it back. A HUD ring around the crosshair shows the cone in real time. A shot fires through a uniformly random point inside the cone. Arrows fly straight for `gravityDelay` (0.25 s), then gravity ramps in over `gravityRampTime` (0.5 s) up to `gravityScale` (0.5) × `Physics.gravity`, and the arrow tilts along its velocity.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- Spread state is owned by `PlayerCombat`, next to the nock state. Before the nock it is at max. It resets to max on aim start and after every shot (shrinking again from the next nock). Time is scaled (it pauses with the game).
- Moving = CharacterController horizontal speed > `aimMoveSpeedThreshold` (0.5 m/s). While moving, progress goes backwards at `aimMoveWidenMultiplier` (1) × the shrink rate (clamped to max). Standing still resumes the shrink.
- The cone is angular (around the crosshair ray). The ring's screen radius is `tan(angle) / tan(vFOV / 2) × Screen.height / 2`, using the live camera FOV, so it stays correct during the aim zoom. UICanvas has no CanvasScaler, so values are in pixels.
- Shot: random direction inside the cone around the crosshair ray (uniform over the disk, `sqrt` radius), cast with the existing `GetAimPoint` filtering. `TryGetAimPoint` (spine correction) keeps the centre ray.
- Pure math (spread step, angle, cone sample, screen radius, gravity factor) in static helpers with EditMode tests. `GameLog` only. Animator untouched.
- Projectile: integrate velocity, sweep along this frame's displacement, rotation = `LookRotation(velocity)`. A stuck arrow uses the impact direction.
- Ring: thin white anti-aliased ring PNG, generated by editor code. It is visible only while aiming, never smaller than the crosshair.

**Ask First:** New input actions. Animator / controller changes. Changing fire gates or cooldown.

**Never:** Trajectory preview, aim compensation for drop, Dexterity-based spread, NPC archers, pooling, a Rigidbody on the projectile.

## Design Notes

Uniform disk sample: `r = angle × sqrt(u)`, `θ = 2πv`, then rotate the ray direction by the yaw / pitch offsets in the camera basis (`Quaternion.AngleAxis(r, axis(θ)) * dir`, with the axis perpendicular to `dir`). Drop at 40 m/s with the defaults (closed form, verified during implementation): ≈0 m at 10 m, ≈0.03 m at 20 m, ≈0.65 m at 40 m, ≈2.5 m at 60 m — gentler than the planning estimate in the I/O matrix (1.5 m at 40 m); raise `gravityScale` to steepen.

Ring: drawn by `AimSpreadRingGraphic` (UI mesh, constant 2 px stroke) instead of the planned generated PNG — a scaled sprite ring's stroke would range from ≈0.3 px (min size) to ≈8 px (5° at aim FOV).
