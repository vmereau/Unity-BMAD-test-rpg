## Intent

**Problem:** The clips drive the fingers with their own muscle curves. On the v2 five-finger hand a clip fist (all
`Stretched` muscles at −1) curls each finger ~270° (measured: MCP ~110°, PIP / DIP ~80° each), so fingertips pass through
the palm (Unarmed Idle Guard, lead jab). Grips differ per clip and none closes on the weapon handle.

**Approach:** Two finger-only masked layers in `Humanoid_Template` (`LeftHand`, `RightHand`, Override, weight 1) that
always replace the clips' fingers with authored poses — relaxed, fist, one-hand grip, two-hand grip, bow grip, shield
hold — picked each frame from the equipped weapon / shield and the combat flag, for the player and NPCs alike.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- One pure rule (`HandPoseRule`, tested) maps (weapon archetype, weapon visual in hand, shield in hand, in combat) to a
  left and a right pose. Same rule for player and NPCs; Animator writes only through `HumanoidAnimationBridge`.
- Pose clips hold finger muscle curves only (no events, no other curves); masks enable only Left / Right Fingers.
- New layers are appended after `UpperBody` (indices 0 / 1 unchanged).
- Poses are tuned on the v2 hand: a closed fist with tips on the palm surface, not inside it; grips wrap the existing
  handles in their current `WeaponSocket` / `LeftWeaponSocket` placement.
- Clips, layers and masks are generated by an idempotent editor builder (values live in code), not hand-edited YAML.

**Ask First:**
- Any change to attack clips, timings, hit events, combat ranges, weapon sockets or archetype grip offsets.
- A pose that cannot wrap a handle without moving the weapon (show the gap first).

**Never:**
- Tool / activity grips (smithing, cooking, carrying), IK, editing the source clips' finger curves, changing the avatar
  muscle limits.

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Left / Right pose |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Out of combat, anything equipped | not in combat, weapon sheathed | Relaxed / Relaxed |
| Unarmed fight (player or NPC brawl with weapon sheathed) | in combat, no weapon visual in hand | Fist / Fist |
| One-hand sword / axe drawn | right-hand archetype, no shield | Relaxed / OneHandGrip |
| Sword + shield drawn | shield in hand | ShieldHold / OneHandGrip |
| Two-hander drawn | `twoHandedGrip` | TwoHandGrip / TwoHandGrip |
| Bow drawn (idle, aim, fire) | `drawnHand` Left | BowGrip / Relaxed |
| No `EquipmentVisuals` on the entity | missing reference | unarmed rule (Fist in combat, else Relaxed) |

## Design Notes

Layers stay at weight 1 and override the clip fingers in every state (relaxed by default): drink, pick-up, swim and
sleep read fine with a relaxed hand, and a ragdoll disables the Animator anyway. "In combat" is the Animator's
`IsInCombat`, not `EquipmentVisuals.IsDrawn`: NPC brawls keep the weapon sheathed while fighting. "Weapon in hand" =
`IsDrawn && ActiveWeaponGO != null`. The bow's right hand stays relaxed (it reads as a string hook); a dedicated draw
pose is later work.

## Design Notes (implementation)

- **Bow moved (user-approved, Ask First):** the riser sat 3–5 cm past the left fingertips (palm-to-bow 65 mm). Only
  `Archetype_Bow.drawnPose.localPosition` changed, (0, 0, 0) → (0.0408, −0.0647, 0.0033): the riser grip now sits where
  the sword handle sits relative to the palm (+1 cm for its depth). Rotation unchanged; arrows unaffected (`BowAimCorrection`
  is calibrated hand-to-hand). The aim clip's pronated wrist keeps the riser across the palm plane (deferred).
- **`HandPoseCapture`** (editor tool, not in the task list) is the pose check: preview-scene close-ups + per-hand palm
  depth, joint curls and handle gaps. Final numbers: fist MCP 78–86° / PIP 77–81° / DIP 69–73°, finger skin ≤ 4.9 mm against
  the palm (contact); grips: middle / ring 3–13 mm from the handle surface, palm 22–28 mm (sword, shield, bow), 40–48 mm
  (two-hander, handle ~1 cm off the palm skin at its socket: fingers still close on it, offset left unchanged).
- **Review patches:** empty hands stay `Relaxed` during a draw / sheathe (no fist flash before WeaponSwap) and while the
  body is down (`bridge.IsSwitchingWeapon` / `IsBaseDown`); the builder validates the controller before writing and
  rebuilds each hand layer at its index; `SetHandPoses` is a silent no-op on a controller without the params.
- **Evidence:** EditMode 1931/1931 pass. Close-ups `D:\claude-scratch\handpose\final\` (+ `final_sheet.png`); in-game
  (StartingTown, player driven through `PlayerCombat`'s draw / attack handlers, frames stepped): `Temp/HandPoses/`
  `ingame_unarmed_guard_1.png`, `ingame_unarmed_jab.png`, `ingame_2hand_idle.png`, `ingame_2hand_swing.png`. In play,
  22 `HandPoseDriver`s ran (player + NPCs), fists L/R = 1 in the fight, `TwoHandGrip` = 3 after the override swap.
